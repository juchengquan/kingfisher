# One record of what happened

**Status:** proposed. Slice (a) is being built; slices (b)–(e) are not.
**Date:** 2026-11-14.
**Occasion:** decided with the owner one question at a time. The goal is that the agent
sees one copy of what happened in its conversation, in one place it can read but never
write, and that this copy follows a rewind.

## Today

A session keeps what happened in three places the agent can reach. The saved tool
results are in `/scratchpad/tool_results/`, inside the scratchpad the agent writes, so
that one subfolder is refused writes by rule: a deny for the file tools, and a rule in
each fence for the shell, which Landlock cannot express inside a folder it grants whole.
deepagents' summariser keeps the conversation it replaced in
`/scratchpad/conversation_history/`, and copies a batch too long to send to
`/scratchpad/large_tool_results/`. Neither of those two follows a rewind, and neither is
the record kingfisher rebuilds a conversation from, which is the turn log in `.harness`.

## The plan

**`/records/` is a new top-level folder, read-only to the agent and renamable by a
deployment's `Layout`.** Only kingfisher writes it. The saved results move under it, as
`/records/tool_results/`, and renaming the scratchpad no longer moves them.

**`/records/conversation.md` is rebuilt from kingfisher's turn log.** It holds only what
summaries removed, along the current path, with a heading for each turn. A tool result
appears as its call and a row like the ledger's -- its id, size and file -- and is never
inlined. It is rebuilt at the start of a turn and right after a summary made mid-turn.
deepagents' summariser stays the default, but `conversation_history/` and
`large_tool_results/` refuse its writes; kingfisher puts a pointer to
`/records/conversation.md` into the summary's text and quiets deepagents' warning about
the refused write. If the newest batch is still over the budget after a summary, the
turn fails with a context overflow. A subagent keeps its summary only.

**A task over deepagents' limit on a human message is refused up front**, and
deepagents' eviction of that message is turned off.

**A rewind moves the saved-result files of the turns it abandons into `.harness/`**, and
a forward rewind moves them back.

**`session.md` loses its sentence about `conversation_history/`**, with the docs that
describe it.

**No backward compatibility.** An older session's `/scratchpad/tool_results/` is neither
moved nor refused.

## Slices

- **(a) The folder.** `Layout.records`, the saved results under it, every route, deny
  rule, fence, prompt and check that named the old folder, and the docs. *Being built.*
- **(b) The conversation.** `/records/conversation.md`, the refused writes to
  deepagents' two folders, the pointer in the summary, and the overflow failure.
- **(c) The long task.** Refusing a task over the human-message limit, and turning
  deepagents' eviction of it off. Independent of (b), so built beside it.
- **(d) Rewinds.** Moving abandoned turns' saved results into `.harness/` and back.
- **(e) The prompt.** Cutting `session.md`'s `conversation_history` sentence, with docs.
